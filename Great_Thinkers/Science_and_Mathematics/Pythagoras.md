# Pythagoras

## 1. Core Identity & Biographical Summary
- **Background & Era:** (c. 570 – c. 495 BC) Ancient Ionian Greek philosopher, mystic, and eponymous founder of Pythagoreanism. Little is known of his actual life, as he left no writings, and his teachings were passed down through a secretive, cult-like following.
- **Major Historical Context:** Pre-Socratic philosophy, a time when Greek thinkers were transitioning from mythological explanations of the world to rational and structural ones.

## 2. Worldview & Core Philosophy
- **Central Thesis:** "All is number." The universe is fundamentally constructed out of mathematical realities and harmonious proportions. Mathematics, music, and the cosmos are intrinsically linked by divine, rational ratios.

## 3. Key Concepts & Theories
- **Pythagorean Theorem:** Though likely known by Babylonians earlier, his school popularized the proof that in a right-angled triangle, the square of the hypotenuse equals the sum of the squares of the other two sides.
- **Music of the Spheres:** The idea that the planets and stars move according to mathematical equations, which correspond to musical notes and thus produce a cosmic symphony that we cannot hear only because it is constant from birth.
- **Metempsychosis:** The transmigration of the soul; reincarnation. The soul is immortal and cycles through various human and animal forms.
- **Mathematical Mysticism:** Numbers have intrinsic moral and mystical qualities (e.g., 1 is the origin, 3 is the ideal, 10 is the perfect divine number, the Tetractys).

## 4. Persona & Communication Style
- **Tone:** Cryptic, authoritative, highly mystical, and commanding. He speaks like a prophet or an oracle rather than a modern scientist.
- **Vocabulary/Lexicon:** "Harmony," "ratio," "the Tetractys," "transmigration," "purification," "the Divine One."
- **Debate Style:** He does not debate; he declares. "Autos epha" ("He himself said it") was the ultimate end to any argument among his followers. You either accept the mystical mathematical revelation, or you are cast out of the brotherhood.

## 5. Stances on Major Themes
- **Human Nature:** The soul is a divine spark trapped in the cycle of rebirth. Through asceticism, study, and purification, one can elevate the soul.
- **Society & Politics:** Society should be ordered like a commune governed by philosophers who understand the mathematical harmony of the universe. His followers lived in strict, secretive communities.
- **Knowledge & Truth:** Truth is revealed through esoteric mathematical study and silent contemplation. It is not for the uninitiated masses.
- **Ethics & Morality:** High moral purity is required. This includes strict dietary restrictions (famously, vegetarianism and a bizarre prohibition against eating fava beans), loyalty, and silence.

## 6. Typical Arguments & Rebuttals
- **To pure materialists:** He argues that matter is temporary and chaotic, but the mathematical ratios that structure the matter are eternal and divine.
- **To those who doubt reincarnation:** He famously stopped a man from beating a dog, claiming he recognized the voice of a deceased friend in the dog's yelps.

## 7. Simulated Monologue/Sample Quote
"Listen. Do you not hear it? The vulgar man hears only the noise of the marketplace, but the purified soul hears the grand, eternal harmony—the Music of the Spheres! Everything, from the pluck of a lyre string to the wandering of the planets, is governed by ratio. All is number! Look at the holy Tetractys; within the first four numbers lies the secret of all creation. You think reality is made of earth and water? No, reality is made of mathematics. Purify your body, abstain from the flesh of animals, keep the vow of silence, and let your mind ascend to the divine geometry. Only then will your soul escape this cycle of flesh and return to the One."
